## Preconditions

## Steps
1. In radaac tab, search and select "Brand Definitions (Fetch)".
2. Add value "236" in "Brand IDs (CSV)".
3. Click Submit.
4. Open downloaded xlsx and save the file.
5. Click Radaac Home (top left).
6. Search and select "Brand Definitions (Patch)".
7. "Choose file" → upload the saved xlsx.
8. Click Submit.
9. Open the downloaded xlsx.
10. Click Radaac Home.
11. Search and select "Brand Definitions (Apply)".
12. "Choose file" → upload the Patch report xlsx.
13. Click Submit.
14. Open the downloaded xlsx.

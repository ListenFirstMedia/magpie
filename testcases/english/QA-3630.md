## Preconditions
- User should be logged in as Michael Kors

## Steps
1. Click Reporting in Top Nav 
2. Select 'Content Performance’ 
3. In the Add Brand by name field enter, Michael Kors and change the perspective to Authorized
4. Select the Last 30 days
5. In the PIck Channel Data select Facebook, Twitter, Instagram and YouTube
6. Under Most Engaging Content, input 5 in Visual Top posts 
7.  For Additional Top Post Table Rows enter 5
8. Check Least Engaging Content and input 5 in Visual Bottom posts 
9. Run the report and review

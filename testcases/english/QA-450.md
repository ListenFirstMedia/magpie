## Preconditions
User receives the Action Alerts email at their inbox.

## Why blocked
Verifying an email's content requires access to the inbox where the Action Alert lands. The chrome MCP session is signed into the LFM web app, not into the recipient's mail client. Reading the email body without an email-MCP connector or a screenshot from the user is not possible.

## Unblock
Either (a) forward the most recent Action Alert email's HTML source to me, or (b) connect a Gmail/Outlook MCP that lets me read the LFIQA mailbox.

## Steps
1. Hover over the Brand dropdown in the top navigation and click Channel.
2. Search for MTV brand and select it.
3. Change the Public Perspective.
4. Review the Instagram Channel tile.
5. Click on the Insights button below the IG channel tile and open the insights page in new tab.
6. Review the Channel page and Insight Page.
7. Click on the Content button below the IG channel tile in brand channel Page.
8. Review the Channel Page and Content Page.

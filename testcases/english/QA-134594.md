# QA-134594 — Brand > Video > Instagram > Public Data

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-134594
- **Priority:** Major
- **Ingested:** 2026-06-27 from Jira (Xray custom fields)

## Preconditions
User should be logged in as Hulu.

## Steps
1.  Hover over the Brand dropdown in the top navigation and click Video.
2. Search for the Hulu brand and select it.
3. Change to the Public Data perspective.
4. Select Instagram channel only.
5. Review the New Video Posts tile in 2 Row.
6. Review the Best Performing Content tile.
7. Review the Tiles(Just for checking that private "Page Video Views" should not included when public perspective selected).
8. Change the Perspective public data to Authorized data.

# QA-137047 — Reporting > TWC > Instagram Public Video View data & Brand Channel Instagram Public Video View data

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-137047
- **Priority:** Major
- **Ingested:** 2026-07-09 from Jira (Xray custom fields)

## Preconditions
- User should be logged in as Hulu.

## Steps
1. Hover over the Reporting dropdown in the top navigation and click TWC.
2. Search for Hulu in the brand search box and click Add Brand.
3. Change the Perspective to Public.
4. Select the date range as Last 7 Days.
5. Expand Content > Video > Channel View in the channel Data section.
6. Verify that Facebook Public Video View, Twitter Public Video View, Instagram Public Video View, and TikTok Public Video View are present and selectable.
7. Select Instagram Public Video View.
8. Click Run Report.
9. Note down the sum of Instagram Public Video View values for all 7 days from the report.
10. Hover over the Brand menu and open Channel in a new tab.
11. Select the Hulu brand.
12. Change the Perspective to Public.
13. Select the date range as Last 7 Days.
14. Note down the Instagram Video View value shown on the Instagram tile.
15. Compare the sum from the TWC report (Step 9) with the value from the Channel page (Step 14).

## Assertions
6. All four Public Video View options (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok) should be visible and selectable in the TWC report.
8(a). Ensure the Instagram Public Video View graph displays correctly.
8(b). Ensure the Instagram Public Video View table displays with the columns "Instagram Public Video View" and "Brand Name".
8(c). Ensure the Instagram Public Video View columns "Date" and "Brand Name" contain values.
8(d). Ensure the Instagram Public Video View columns are sortable.
15. The Instagram Public Video View sum from the TWC report should match the Instagram Video View value shown on the Channel page for the same brand, perspective, and date range.

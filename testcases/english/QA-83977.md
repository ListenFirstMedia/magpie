# Steps

1. Hover over 'Reporting' in the top nav
2. Click 'Data Studio' in the dropdown
3. Click on the text box displayed near the "Add a Brand" option.
4. Type and add the following Brands 'Star Wars' and 'Michael Kors' in the add a brand section
5. Change 'Michael Kors' perspective to 'Authorized'
6. Click the 'Select Metrics' button
7. Click on the 'New Video Posts' subheader under the 'Posts' header in the top-level controls
8. Click the checkbox near the 'Facebook Video Posts', and 'Twitter Video Posts'
9. Click 'Go' button
10. Hover over the Graph for 'Star Wars'
11. Hover over the Graph for 'Michael Kors'
12. Click 'Show Configuration button'
13. Adjust the horizontal scroll bar in the metric table

# Assertions

- 7. Ensure that only the corresponding metrics are expanded under 'New Video Posts' header in the page metrics tree
- 10. Ensure the tooltips display the P icon next to the brand name
- 11. Ensure the tooltips do not display any icon near the brand name
- 13. Ensure the 'Metric' and 'Brand' columns remain fixed and other columns are moving

# QA-99380 — Brand > Content - Daily Post Analysis Modal - Graph Display & Behavior

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-99380
- **Description:** Ensure the Graph display & behavior of the Daily Analysis Modal on the Brand Content page.

## Preconditions
- User logged in.
- Brand with sufficient daily-aggregated data (Adam Orfei or MTV).

## Steps
1. Navigate to Brand > Content for the brand.
2. Pick a date range with multi-day data (Last 30 Days or similar).
3. Click a Daily Post Analysis surface that opens the Daily Analysis Modal.
4. Switch the modal to Graph (Line) view if Table view is default.
5. Verify the graph renders all in-range days, x-axis shows date labels, y-axis shows metric values.
6. Hover a bar / point. Confirm tooltip with date + metric value.
7. Switch metric in modal dropdown; verify graph repopulates.
8. Look for endash patterns on TikTok dates (DATA-12209).

## Assertions
- A1: Modal opens with a Line/Graph view option.
- A2: Graph shows all in-range days with axis labels.
- A3: Hover tooltip displays values.
- A4: Switching metric repopulates graph.
- A5: No unexpected endash for non-TikTok dates.

## Preconditions
Logged into Mixpanel: https://mixpanel.com/project/1485629/view/35285/app/boards#id=5253657

## Steps
1. Click Boards → Dashboard Board.
2. Review **Page Viewed** event.
3. Review **Channel Selector Switched** event.
4. Review **Dashboard Edited** event.
5. Review **File Export** event.
6. Review **Date Range Changed** event.
7. Review **Dashboard Share Configuration** event.
8. Review **Short Link Generated** event.
9. Review **Bulk Share Dashboard Configuration** event.
10. Review **Dashboard Shared** event.
11. Review **Dashboard Deleted** event.
12. Review **Dashboard Loaded** event.
13. Review **Dashboard Created** event.

## Assertions
For every event (steps 2–13), the rendered tile cells must contain no `Null`, `Not set`, `Undefined`, or anomaly characters/words. Additionally:
- **A_extra (step 4):** "Dashboard Edited" event's Tile Order Property displays in the format `["MTV (Brand: Paid) Authorized Data - Active Ads"]` — should not be empty or "undefined".

# QA-134507 — BrandSet > Ranking > Instagram > Public Video View & Average Public Video View

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-134507
- **Priority:** Major
- **Ingested:** 2026-07-09 from Jira (Xray custom fields)

## Preconditions
- User should be logged in as Viacom.

## Steps
1. Hover over the Brand Sets dropdown in the top navigation and click Rankings.
2. Select 'LF // TV // Episodic' Brand Set.
3. Select 'Instagram' channel only.
4. Click on the 'Rank' dropdown.
5. Select 'Public Video Views' from the dropdown.
6. Click on the Public Video Views column header twice time.
7. Take the Sum value from the aggregate table and calculate the Average of Public Video Views (Sum ÷ Post Count for posts that have data). Then switch the toggle from Sum to Avg and note the Average value from the aggregate table.
8. Select 'Average Public Video Views' from the dropdown.
9. Click on the Average Public Video Views column header twice time.

## Assertions
4. Ensure that both Public Video Views and Average Public Video Views metrics are displayed in the data.
5(a). Ensure that the Public Video Views column is present in the brand table and that the ranking is determined based on the Public Video Views metric.
5(b). Ensure that it does not include any other (except selected rank) metric from the rank dropdown option.
5(c). Ensure that the sum of all brand Public Video Views is equal to the aggregate sum.
5(d). Ensure that the average of all brand Public Video Views is equal to the aggregate average.
6. Public Video Views column sort correctly in descending and ascending order.
7. Ensure the calculated Average of public video view must equal to Aggregate table Average.
8(a). Ensure that the Average Public Video Views column is present in the brand table and that the ranking is determined based on the Average Public Video Views metric.
8(b). Ensure that it does not include any other (except selected rank) metric from the rank dropdown option.
8(c). Ensure that the Aggregate Sum toggle is not supported for Average Public Views.
8(d). Ensure that the average of all brand Average Public Video Views is equal to the aggregate average of Average Public Views.
9. Average Public Video Views column sort correctly in descending and ascending order.

# QA-96665 — Brand Insights - Threads - Basic View

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-96665
- **Priority:** Blocker (P1)
- **Description:** Ensures the basic view and BPC functionality for Threads Channel in Brand Insights Page.

## Preconditions
- User should be logged in as Max

## Steps
1. Click Brand in the top nav and select Insights
2. Click brand dropdown, type "Max" brand
3. Click Threads icon in the channels selector and click apply
4. Change perspective to 'Public'

## Assertions
- A1 (step 3): Ensure the Threads channel icon appears in the Single channel selection, positioned next to LinkedIn channel, with a separator in between.
- A2 (step 3): Ensure the following big number tiles are displayed in the first row: Total Followers, Follower Growth, and Fan Growth Rate
- A3 (step 3): Ensure the following big number tiles are displayed in the second row: New Posts and Engagements.
- A4 (step 3): Ensure the following big number tiles are displayed in the third row: Engagement Rate and Views
- A5 (step 3): Ensure the BPC tile is displayed in the fourth row.
- A6 (step 3): Ensure the following metrics are displayed in the BPC posts and sort by dropdown: Engagements, Likes, Replies, Reposts, Quotes, Shares, and Views.
- A7 (step 3): Ensure the BPC contains five posts.
- A8 (step 3): Ensure the BPC is sorted by default according to 'Engagements.'
- A9 (step 4): Ensure that the Threads channel should not be available (when perspective = Public)

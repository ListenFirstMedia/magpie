## Preconditions
User logged in as the **Adam Orfei** account.

## Description
Verifies the calculation of YouTube engagements at the post level: `YouTube Video Engagements` should equal the sum of `YouTube Video Likes` + `YouTube Video Comments` for every post.

## Steps
1. Hover over **Reporting** in the top nav, click **Data Studio**.
2. Click the **Post Level** selection.
3. In the **Add Brand** textbox, type and choose `MTV`.
4. Click **Interval and Aggregate** (TBD — explore).
5. Click the **Select Metrics** button.
6. In the search box, select these metrics:
   - YouTube Video Engagements
   - YouTube Video Likes
   - YouTube Video Comments
7. Click **Go**.
8. For every post row, compute `Likes + Comments` and compare to `Engagements`.

## Assertions
- **A1 (per-row math identity):** For every row in the post-level report, `YouTube Video Engagements == YouTube Video Likes + YouTube Video Comments`.
  - type: numeric (per row, after stripping thousands separators)
  - source: post-level data table
  - normalization: parse cells as integers; strip commas; treat blank or `–` cells as 0 for the math but flag any case where one of the three cells is missing while the others are populated
  - tolerance: 0 (exact integer match)

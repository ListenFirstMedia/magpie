## Description
Validates that Facebook Gallery posts for HBO Max do not retain any Reels association that causes duplication of Reel posts in Brand Content. When filtering Content Type = Gallery, only gallery posts must appear, and the reels contained in those gallery posts must not appear again (no duplicated Reel posts) when filtering Content Type = Video for the same date range.

## Preconditions
The user should be logged in as HBO MAX. Brand: HBO Max. Channel: Facebook. Dataset: Impressions. Date range: 11 Apr 2026 to 15 Apr 2026.

## Steps
1. Click Brand in the top navigation and select Content.
2. Type and select HBO Max in the brand dropdown.
3. Select Facebook channel only.
4. Set the date range from 11 Apr 2026 to 15 Apr 2026.
5. Click the dataset dropdown and select the Impressions dataset.
6. Click the filter dropdown and apply Content Type: Gallery, then click Apply.
7. From the filtered results, identify and note one Gallery post that contains multiple Reels within the same gallery/container post (capture identifiers like post URL/content id/text/date).
8. Open Brand → Content in a new browser tab.
9. In the new tab, repeat steps 2–5 (same brand, channel, dataset, and date range).
10. Click the filter dropdown and apply Content Type: Video, then click Apply.
11. Search/review the Video-filtered results to confirm that the Reels contained/associated within the Gallery post noted in step 7 are not present here.

## Assertions
- A1 (step 6a): Ensure the page refreshes and the post count updates after applying Content Type: Gallery.
- A2 (step 6b): Ensure only Gallery posts are displayed after applying Content Type: Gallery.
- A3 (step 7a): Ensure the selected post is a Gallery post and contains multiple Reels within the same gallery/container post.
- A4 (step 10a): Ensure the page refreshes and the post count updates after applying Content Type: Video.
- A5 (step 10b): Ensure only Video posts are displayed after applying Content Type: Video.
- A6 (step 11a): Ensure the Reels that are contained/associated within the Gallery post from step 7 are not present in the Video-filtered results (i.e., no duplication of Reel posts due to Gallery association).

## Cleanup
None — read-only verification, no mutation.

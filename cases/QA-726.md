## Steps
1. Brand → Content.
2. Click search for a brand.
3. Enter `Disney Channel`.
4. Select the brand.
5. Select YouTube channel only, click Apply.
6. Click the tag button on the first post.
7. Attempt to add a tag > 100 characters.
8. Review the character limit.

## Assertions
- **A1 (step 8):** Add-tag message box states `"Please enter up to 100 Characters"`.
- **A2 (step 8):** Total typed cannot exceed 100 characters.

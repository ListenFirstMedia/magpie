## Preconditions
User logged in as Michael Kors.

## Steps
1. Brand → Insights.
2. Search and select Michael Kors brand.
3. Review Total Followers tile.
4. Export → CSV.
5. Data Visualization dropdown → Share.
6. Unselect TikTok channel.

## Assertions
- A1 (step 3a): Total Followers header format `Total Followers: 93.2M (-<1%)`.
- A2 (step 3b): Legend order: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok.
- A3 (step 4a): CSV filename `Brand-Tab-Tile-YYYY-MM-DD(Start)-YYYY-MM-DD(End).csv`.
- A4 (step 4b): CSV headers: Brand Name, Channel, Total Followers.
- A5 (step 5): Pie chart shows percentages.
- A6 (step 6): Pie chart updates; TikTok removed from Legend.

# QA-134184 — Brand > Insights - Interval selection - Quarterly

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-134184
- **Priority:** Critical (P2)
- **Description:** Ensure Quarterly Interval and Multiple Quarters behavior in Brand > Insights date range selector.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Hulu

## Steps
1. Click Brand top nav
2. Select Insights
3. Date Range → Interval dropdown
4. Select Quarterly
5. Jan to June quarter selection
6. Partial quarter range (e.g., Feb – May)
7. Date range 1 Oct – 31 Dec
8. Date range 1 Sep – 31 Dec
9. Date range 1 Jul – 31 Dec

## Assertions
- A1 (3): Quarterly option shown below Monthly
- A2 (4a): Start/End date labels display as Quarters
- A3 (4b): Selecting a quarter auto-selects last complete 3-month period (e.g., Jan–Mar)
- A4 (5): Multiple quarters select correctly (Jan–Mar & Apr–Jun)
- A5 (6): Partial quarter resolves to full quarters (Feb–May → Jan–Mar & Apr–Jun)
- A6 (7): Only Q4 (Oct–Dec) displayed for 1 Oct–31 Dec range
- A7 (8): Q3 (Jul–Sep) & Q4 (Oct–Dec) displayed for 1 Sep–31 Dec — non-obvious: range extends backward to include partial Q3 (Philip corrected Suhail's Sep–Dec = Q4 assumption on APPS-58615)
- A8 (9): Q3 (Jul–Sep) & Q4 (Oct–Dec) displayed for 1 Jul–31 Dec

# QA-109919 — Brand > Content - Sentiment Comments limit - CSV

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-109919
- **Priority:** Critical
- **Ingested:** 2026-07-13 from Jira (Xray custom fields)

## Preconditions
User logged in as Amazon Prime Video

## Steps
1. Click Brand in the top nav and click Content in the dropdown
2. Click the Brand dropdown and Type and select Amazon Prime Video
3. Select date range Mar. 03, 2025 to Mar. 06, 2025
4. Click the Sentiment button
5. Scroll down to the Posts table
6. Click on 'Read Comments' on the first post
7. Click on the Export Comments button and click CSV in the dropdown
8. Click the 'Ok' button on the 'Sentiment Export Request' pop-up

## Assertions
6(a). Ensure the header should display as "Post Comments (2000)"
6(b). Ensure that below the header, the following message should display "2000 Sample Comments. To view all possible comments, please export your result as on screen display is limited to 2,000 samples."
8(a). Ensure the CSV auto downloads itself
8(b). Ensure the CSV export has more than 2000 comments

# QA-109920 — Brand > Content - Sentiment Comments limit - Positive Classification Donut - CSV

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-109920
- **Priority:** Critical (P2)
- **Description:** Ensures Sentiment Comments limit in CSV export of sample comments popup in positive Classification donut chart.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Amazon Prime Video

## Steps
1. Hover Brand → Content
2. Click Brand dropdown, type and select Amazon Prime Video
3. Select date range Apr 01, 2025 to Apr 07, 2025
4. Click Sentiment button
5. Hover on Positive classification in the 'Classification' donut chart
6. Click on Read in the pop-up
7. Click Export button and click CSV in the dropdown
8. Click 'Ok' button on the 'Sentiment Export Request' pop-up

## Assertions
- A1 (step 6): Pop-up opens
- A2 (step 6): Message displays: "2,000 Sample Comments. To view all possible comments, please export your result as on screen display is limited to 2,000"
- A3 (step 8): CSV export has more than 2000 comments

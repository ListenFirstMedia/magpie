# QA-121158 — Brand Content - Instagram - Collaborated Total Filter Functionality

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-121158
- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Ensures the Collaborated Total filter functionality in Brand Content tab.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Hulu

## Steps
1. Click Brand in the top navigation and click the Content tab
2. Type and select "Hulu" brand from the list
3. Click the Filter dropdown
4. Click on Collaborated Total and click on any number (e.g. 2).
5. Click on Apply Filter
6. Observe the posts Collaborated count

## Assertions
- A1 (step 3): Ensure the Collaborated Total filter is between Collaborated and Collaborated Name.
- A2 (step 4): Ensure the Collaborated Total filter options are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5.
- A3 (step 5): Ensure the Posts header count is updated.
- A4 (step 5): Ensure 'Collaborated' posts only displays in the post table.
- A5 (step 6): Ensure the Collaborated Total filter count and posts Collaborated count should be same

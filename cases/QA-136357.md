## Description
This test case validates the individual posts and Sum/Average row display behavior for Reach, Organic Reach, and Paid Reach metrics on Facebook Reel posts within the Impressions dataset. For the specified date range, Reach metrics must render consistently as en-dash (–) in Each Post, N/A in Sum, and en-dash (–) in Average.

## Preconditions
The user should be logged in as HBO MAX. Brand: HBO Max. Channel: Facebook. Dataset: Impressions. Date range: 15 Nov 2025 to 10 May 2026. Filter: Publish type = Reel.

## Steps
1. Click Brand in the top navigation and select Content.
2. Type and select HBO Max in the brand dropdown.
3. Select Facebook channel only.
4. Set the date range from 15 Nov 2025 to 10 May 2026.
5. Click the dataset dropdown and select the Impressions dataset.
6. Click the filter dropdown and apply Publish type: Reel, then click Apply.

## Assertions
- A1 (step 6a): Ensure the page refreshes and the post Count updates based on the filter.
- A2 (step 6b): Ensure only Facebook Reel posts are displayed after applying Publish type: Reel.
- A3 (step 6c): Ensure that for each displayed Facebook Reel post, the values for Reach, Organic Reach, and Paid Reach are shown as an en dash (–) at the post level.
- A4 (step 6d): Ensure the Sum value displays N/A for Reach, Organic Reach, and Paid Reach.
- A5 (step 6e): Ensure the Average value displays an en dash (–) for Reach, Organic Reach, and Paid Reach.

## Cleanup
None — read-only verification, no mutation.

## Notes
Spec-documented exception: for date ranges ≤ 14 Nov 2025, Reach/Organic Reach/Paid Reach may appear inconsistently (numeric for some posts, en-dash for others) — do NOT raise a bug for that pre-cutoff inconsistency. Only treat as an issue if the post-cutoff behavior (15 Nov 2025 onward, i.e. within this test's own date range) is not met.

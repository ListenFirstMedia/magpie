## Steps
1. Hover over 'Reporting' in the top navigation bar
2. Click 'Data Studio' in the dropdown menu
3. Review the "Data Last Updated: Timestamp"
4. In the 'Add Brand' textbox, type and choose 'MTV' and 'Star Wars' brands
5. Click the 'Select Metrics' button near the Page Level Metrics section
6. Click the Filter search input field, type and select 'Facebook Total Fans', 'Twitter Total Followers', and 'Instagram Total Followers' in the Page metrics section
7. Click the 'Go' button
8. Review the Graph Report
9. Click the Graph type dropdown
10. Change the Graph type to 'Pie'
11. Click the Graph metric dropdown and select 'Instagram Total Followers'
12. Change the Graph type to 'Bar'

## Preconditions
User logged in as **Adam Orfei**.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in top-right corner.
2. Click **Data Collection**.
3. In My Brands, click **Scorpion**.
4. In Channels, click **Facebook**.
5. In Pages, click the **Scorpion** page.
6. Click the **Expand (˅)** icon to display connected Ad Accounts and statuses.

## Assertions
- **A1:** When only ONE Ad Account is connected, the overall status reflects that Ad Account's status (e.g., "Collecting" or "Not Collecting").
- **A2:** When MULTIPLE Ad Accounts are connected, the overall summary status remains **empty**.
- **A3:** Status is shown only for each Ad Account ID row (no separate overall data-feed status).

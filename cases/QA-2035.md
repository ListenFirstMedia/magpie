# QA-2035 — Brand Sentiment - CSV & GS

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-2035
- **Priority:** Blocker (P1)
- **Description:** Ensure CSV and Google Sheets export of Brand > Content Sentiment work and match.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Viacom

## Steps
1. Click Brand in top nav → Click Content in dropdown
2. Type MTV; select MTV brand from list
3. Click the sentiment button
4. Click Sentiment Export button
5. Click OK button
6. Open corresponding email and download attached CSV export
7. Click Export button and switch toggle to Google Sheets
8. Click OK button
9. Open corresponding email and open attached Google Sheets export

## Assertions
- A1 (step 6): CSV filename format: Brand-Tab-YYYY-MM-DD-YYYY-MM-DD-comments-sentiment.csv
- A2 (step 6): Columns included: Comment Classified, Comment Emotion, Topics (Topic 1, Topic 2, ...)
- A3 (step 6): Expected date range displays in the export
- A4 (step 6): Date in MM/(D)D/YYYY format
- A5 (step 6): Day of Week in DOW format
- A6 (step 6): Time in HH:MM XM PST format
- A7 (step 9): CSV data matches Google Sheets data

# QA-129608 — Handle Abnormally High Response Rate – Aggregate Value Calculation Across multiple channels

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-129608
- **Priority:** Blocker
- **Description:** Ensure the aggregate Response Rate is calculated correctly by excluding days without Total Followers data and using the correct aggregate formula across multiple channels.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Wasserman.

## Steps
1. Click Reporting from the top navigation bar.
2. Select Time Window Comparison from the Reporting menu.
3. Add the brand "FIA World Endurance Championship (FIAWEC)".
4. Select Absolute Date Range and choose Aggregate as the interval.
5. Set the date range to Feb 09, 2026 – Feb 15, 2026.
6. Click By Channel, select Related Nodes, and configure each of the following channels (one at a time):
   - **Instagram**: Audience & Growth > Total Followers; Content > Post, Engagements (Public Only), Organic Response Rate.
   - **Twitter**: Audience & Growth > Total Followers; Content > Post, Engagements, Response Rate.
   - **Facebook**: Audience & Growth > Total Fans; Content > Post, Engagements, Response Rate.
   - **TikTok**: Audience & Growth > Total Followers; Content > Post, Engagements, Response Rate.
   - **YouTube**: Audience & Growth > Total Subscribers; Content > Post, Engagements, Response Rate.
8. Click Run Report.
9. Select Export and select Google Sheet.

## Assertions
- A8 (step 8): Report loads successfully for Aggregate values with no errors.
- A9a (step 9): Aggregate Engagements value and number of posts match Exported data.
- A9b (step 9): UI-displayed Aggregate Response Rate equals Exported Response Rate.
- A9c (step 9): Calculated Aggregate Response Rate matches both UI + Exported data.

## Formula
- Aggregate Response Rate = (Aggregate Engagements / Aggregate Total Footprint) × 100
- Aggregate Footprint = Aggregate Posts × Aggregate Total Followers
- Exclude days without Total Followers data.

## Notes
- Use TWC `th.prev`/`th.next` JS-fallback (known-quirks).

## Assertions
4. Ensure the Sum of the Organic Impressions, Paid Impressions value should be equal to the Impressions Value  
5. Ensure the Sum of the Organic Reach, Promoted Reach value should be equal to Reach Value  
6. Ensure the division of Engagements and Impressions is equal to the Engagement Rate  
7. Ensure the division of Engagements and Reach is equal to the Engaged User Rate  
9. Ensure the Sum of the Organic Impressions, Paid Impressions value should be equal to the Impressions Value  
10. Ensure the Sum of the Organic Reach, Promoted Reach value should be equal to Reach Value  
11. Ensure the division of Engagements and Impressions is equal to the Engagement Rate  
11. Ensure Engaged User rate displays endash

Organic Reach + Promoted Reach = Reach

Engagements / Impressions = Engagement Rate  
Engagements/ Reach = Engaged User Rate

## Steps
1. Click Settings in the top right corner and click Data collection  
2. Search the Michael Kors brand name and select it  
3. In the Channel column, select Instagram  
4. Review the 'Data Collection Summary' page and record the status of every feed shown  
5. Export the CSV file from the same page  
6. Compare the status of each feed in the CSV against the status recorded from the UI  
7. Repeat steps 2 to 6 for a brand on a second channel with a mix of Collecting and Not Collecting feeds

## Assertions
6. Ensure the status of every feed matches between the UI and the exported CSV, row for row  
6. Ensure the feed count in the CSV matches the number of feeds displayed on the UI  
6. Ensure the Dormant indicator in the export corresponds to a feed the UI still shows as Collecting  
7. Ensure the match holds across channels and is not limited to a single channel or feed type

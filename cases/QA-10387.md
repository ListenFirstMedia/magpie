## Preconditions
User is logged in. A brand with Impressions and Video Views chart tiles is accessible on Brand > Insights.

## Steps
1. Navigate to Brand > Insights for a brand with Authorized data (Hulu has FB/IG private data).
2. Locate the Impressions chart tile.
3. Click the tile-level export menu (kebab / Export icon) → choose PNG.
4. Wait for the PNG to download.
5. Locate the Video Views chart tile.
6. Click the tile-level export menu → choose PNG.
7. Wait for the PNG to download.
8. Open both PNGs and verify the chart content and headers.

## Assertions
A1. Both PNG files download with sensible filenames including the brand name + tile name + date range.
A2. Each PNG renders the chart with title (Impressions / Video Views), axes, legend, and the actual chart bars/lines visible.
A3. The PNG shows the same data as on-screen (sum/avg row in the tile aligns with PNG axes).
A4. No render errors or blank canvases.

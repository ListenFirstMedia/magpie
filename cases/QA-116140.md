# QA-116140 — Brand Sentiment - Sentiment Export CTA

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-116140
- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Ensures a Sentiment Export CTA appears only when sentiment is enabled. Upon click a modal pops up similar to Brand > Content Export.

## Preconditions
- User logged in as Adam Orfei

## Steps
1. Click Brand in Top nav and Click the Content in the dropdown
2. Type MTV Select MTV Brand from the list
3. Click the Sentiment button
4. Click the Sentiment Export button
5. Click the Cancel button

## Assertions
- A1 (step 3): Ensure that the Sentiment Export button appears only when Sentiment is enabled.
- A2 (step 3): Ensure that the Sentiment Export button appears to the right of the Sentiment button.
- A3 (step 4): Ensure that the popup message displays "We're hard at work preparing your export. While some exports can finish quickly, larger exports may take longer to complete. Once it's ready, your export will automatically download or open. You can also find the link to download the export in our app notifications menu, bell icon, and in an email to <EMAIL>." after the export option is selected
- A4 (step 5): Ensure that the message closes

## Preconditions
User logged in. A brand with Stories Impressions data is available (Hulu has IG/FB Stories with private/authorized data).

## Steps
1. Navigate to Brand > Stories for Hulu (URL `app.lfmdev.in/#explore/brand/stories?...`).
2. Confirm the page loads and an Impressions tile is visible.
3. Locate the tile-level export menu (kebab / Export icon / dropdown) on the Impressions tile.
4. Click Export → PNG.
5. Wait for the PNG to download.
6. Open the PNG and verify chart content + header + legend + date range.

## Assertions
A1. Brand > Stories page loads with at least one Impressions tile rendered.
A2. The Impressions tile has a per-tile Export affordance with a PNG option (similar to QA-20988 / QA-10387 pattern).
A3. Clicking PNG triggers a download.
A4. Saved filename matches `{Brand}-Stories-Impressions-{ChartType}-{from}-{to}.png` (Rule 6 end-to-end on disk).
A5. PNG embeds Brand name header, chart title (Impressions), chart data, legend, and date range.
A6. No render errors or blank canvases.

## Steps

2. Click 'Add a New User'.
3. Enter First name + Last name.
4. Enter email.
5. Enter phone + job title.
6. Add User.
7. Log into Mixpanel (Dev).
8. Click Users section.
9. Search the new user name.
10. Click into profile.

# QA-95067 — Brand Audience > LinkedIn - Followers By Country & Followers By Region tile Hovering Functionality

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-95067
- **Description:** Verify hover functionality on Followers By Country / Followers By Region tiles on the LinkedIn Brand Audience page.

## Preconditions
- User logged in.
- Brand with LinkedIn data available (UCLA verified working).

## Steps
1. Navigate to Brand > Audience > LinkedIn for a LinkedIn-data brand.
2. Locate the Followers By Country tile (map view).
3. Hover over a country region on the map. Confirm a tooltip appears with country name and follower count / share.
4. Locate the Followers By Region tile (map view).
5. Hover over a region. Confirm a tooltip appears with region name and follower count / share.
6. Re-probe APPS-58574 layout misalignment via DOM measurement.

## Assertions
- A1: Followers By Country tile renders.
- A2: Hover over country shows tooltip with country + value.
- A3: Followers By Region tile renders.
- A4: Hover over region shows tooltip with region + value.
- A5: APPS-58574 layout state captured.

## Probes (open bugs)
- APPS-58574 (Trivial) — LinkedIn Brand>Audience cards misaligned.

# QA-92735 — Brand > Audience - LinkedIn - Basic View

- **Source:** https://listenfirstmedia.atlassian.net/browse/QA-92735
- **Priority:** Blocker (P1)
- **Description:** Verifies the basic view of the LinkedIn Audience tab — card layout, alignment, and rendering.

## Preconditions
- User logged in with access to a brand having LinkedIn audience data (UCLA per bug; MTV / Adam Orfei reasonable).

## Steps
1. Brand → Audience.
2. Type & select brand (UCLA if available; else MTV).
3. Select LinkedIn channel only.
4. Confirm default Basic view loads.
5. Inspect first row of audience cards: Total Followers, Net Followers, New Followers (etc.) — geometry, alignment, single-line vs two-line.
6. Scroll to view Followers By Country / Region / Industry tiles.

## Assertions
- A1: LinkedIn Audience page loads with cards rendered.
- A2 (probe): First-row cards align on a single line (APPS-58574 reports they break to two lines on UCLA).
- A3: Tile titles and counts correct (non-zero where data exists).
- A4: Layout visually correct (no overlap, no truncation).

## Probes (open bugs)
- APPS-58574 (Trivial, In Progress) — Brand Audience LinkedIn first row cards misaligned, separated into two lines (UCLA).

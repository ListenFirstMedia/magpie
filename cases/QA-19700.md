## Assertions
4. Ensure the Sum of the  Organic Views and Paid Views value should be equal to Video Views Value  
5. Ensure the division of Engagements and video views should be eq to Video Response Rate

Common formula:  
Organic Views + Paid Views = Video Views  
Video Response Rate = Engagements/Video Views

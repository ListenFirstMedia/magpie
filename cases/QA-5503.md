## Preconditions
User logged in as **Disney Entertainment Television**.

## Steps
1. Brand → Insights.
2. Brand dropdown → Disney Channel.
3. Click YouTube icon in channels selector, click Apply.
4. Click Sort By dropdown in Best Performing Content (BPC).
5. Select `Comments` from Sort By in BPC.

## Assertions
- **A1 (step 3a):** YouTube icon appears in Single channel selection BEFORE LinkedIn and AFTER Cross-channels, with a separator between Cross-channels and per-channel icons.
- **A2 (step 3b):** Graph-type dropdown + Save-to-Dashboard dropdown display on all big-number tiles EXCEPT Total Subscribers (which has only Save-to-Dashboard).
- **A3 (step 3c):** Row 1 tiles: Total Subscribers, New Subscribers, New Posts.
- **A4 (step 3d):** Row 2 tiles: Likes, Comments.
- **A5 (step 3e):** Row 3: Video Views; last row: BPC.
- **A6 (step 3f):** Export button NOT displayed on any big-number tile.
- **A7 (step 4a):** Sort By dropdown options: Video Views, Likes, Comments.
- **A8 (step 4b):** `Video Views` is the default Sort By option.
- **A9 (step 5):** `Comments` is highlighted in yellow on every BPC post.
